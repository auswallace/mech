# Craft notes

- **Start from the mechanism, then add the shell.** Get the moving parts right in an empty scene. Add covers, then the outer body last.
- **Real kinematics.** Derive positions from the model. For a piston: `s = r cos a + sqrt(L² - r² sin² a)`. Set phases so the model's own timing (for example a firing order) agrees with the geometry, and check one case by hand.
- **Detail that belongs.** Fins on things that get hot, screws at corners of covers, a seam where two castings meet, a notch on anything that spins so the spin is visible.
- **Tone hierarchy.** Structure `hi`, secondary `mid`, rubber and voids `lo`. The watched parts are `lit`. Heat is the one warm colour, driven by load.
- **Ghost levels.** Covers 0.06 to 0.08. Context far from the subject 0.03 to 0.05. Parts near the subject that block the view 0.1 to 0.25.
- **Frame the subject.** After a `focus`, the subject should fill 55 to 70 percent of the stage. Use `pad` to tune.
- **Stage hints are claims.** Each one states something the reader can see in that stage.
- **Check list before you ship:** no console errors, both themes, every stage captured and read, readout numbers match the model, caption states any slow motion and any generic shell.
