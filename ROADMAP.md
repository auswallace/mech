# Roadmap

## Done
- 3D kit with an isometric camera, two looks (real and line), ghosting, exploded views, eased camera moves.
- Shapes: box, cylinder, tube, lathe, torus, volute, loft, profile, plate with holes.
- Teaching layer: part info with click to learn, labels, flow dots, guided lesson, quiz.
- Idle tour, part audit (count and floating parts), capture and contact-sheet scripts.
- Skill workflow: intake questions, fact sheet, storyboard, subject types, self-review, lessons file.
- Examples: twin-turbo V8 in an SUV and a bicycle derailleur (machine), office tower (structure), small language model (abstract), DNS lookup (conversation).
- Accuracy: facts list with statuses and sources, a Sources button on the card, model checks, a second-check procedure.
- Audit: floating parts, hidden labels, tag sizes, failed checks, unlinked facts. Capture saves every lesson step.
- Real parts: toothed gears, chain or belt paths round wheels, links, springs.
- Light and dark theme button. Test prompts with pass criteria in `evals/`.

## Next
- More parts: bearings, pumps, valves, motors, circuit boards, connectors, bolts with real heads.
- A TLS handshake example, with a what-if case for a bad certificate.
- Voice narration made at build time with an open model.
- Collision audit: no pipe or part passes through another, at rest and apart.
- Castings with fillets, and cuts that show a solid face.
- A fourth subject type: process (input, steps, output).

## Later
- Exports: PNG per stage, looping GIF or video, one-page PDF handout.
- A scene written as data (parts, stages, model) instead of code.
- Narration text per lesson step, ready for voice-over.
